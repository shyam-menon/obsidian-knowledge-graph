**Title:** Principle of Least Privilege

**Idea:** Every caller — human user or automated service — should have access only to the minimum resources and actions they actually need. This limits the "blast radius" of a compromised credential: an account with narrow, read-only permissions causes far less damage than an admin-level credential.

**Why it matters:** Security breaches are often inevitable given enough time. Least privilege is a containment strategy — it bounds the damage when something goes wrong rather than trying to prevent all compromise.

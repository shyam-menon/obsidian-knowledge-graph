**Title:** Anomaly Detection from Traffic Baselines

**Idea:** Once a baseline of normal API traffic is established through logs, deviations become detectable signals: spikes in failed auth, unusual geographic sources, sudden high volume on rarely-used endpoints. Anomaly detection converts raw logs into actionable security intelligence.

**Why it matters:** Rule-based detection (e.g., rate limits) catches known patterns. Anomaly detection catches unknown or novel attack patterns by flagging statistical deviations from established norms.

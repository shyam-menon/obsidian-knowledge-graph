Title: Agentic RAG

Agentic RAG extends traditional RAG by introducing agents that can plan, reason, and dynamically decide how to retrieve and use information.

Instead of a fixed pipeline, the system:
- Decides what to retrieve
- Uses tools dynamically
- Iterates based on intermediate results

Why it matters:
- Enables complex workflows
- Improves reasoning and adaptability
- Supports multi-step problem solving

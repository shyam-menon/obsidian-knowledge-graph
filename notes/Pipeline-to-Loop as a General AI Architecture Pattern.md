**Title:** Pipeline-to-Loop as a General AI Architecture Pattern

**Idea:** The shift from one-shot pipelines to iterative feedback loops is not specific to RAG. It reflects a broader architectural evolution in AI systems: from rigid, stateless sequences toward systems that observe outputs, make decisions, and self-correct. This pattern appears in RLHF, tool-using agents, and autonomous AI workflows.

**Why it matters:** Recognizing this as a general principle helps architects anticipate where other AI pipelines will evolve. Any system where output quality is hard to guarantee upfront is a candidate for loop-based, feedback-driven redesign.

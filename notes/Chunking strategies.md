Title: Chunking Strategies

Chunking determines how documents are split before embedding.

Trade-offs:
- Small chunks → better precision, worse context
- Large chunks → better context, worse retrieval accuracy

Types:
- Fixed-size
- Semantic chunking
- Hierarchical chunking

**Title:** Specification Precision in AI Systems

**Idea:**  
Specification precision is the ability to define tasks clearly with explicit inputs, outputs, constraints, and edge cases so that AI systems can execute them reliably.

**Why it matters:**  
AI systems follow instructions literally. Clear specifications directly improve correctness and reduce ambiguity-driven errors. Poor instructions lead to unpredictable or incorrect results.

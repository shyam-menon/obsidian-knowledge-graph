**Title:** Token Economics in AI Systems

**Idea:**  
Token economics refers to estimating and optimizing the cost of AI systems based on token usage, model pricing, and workload scale.

**Why it matters:**  
Efficient cost management ensures that AI systems remain viable and scalable in production. Uncontrolled token usage can make systems expensive and unsustainable in production.

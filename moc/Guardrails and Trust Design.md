**Title:** Guardrails and Trust Design

**Idea:**  
AI systems must be designed with constraints that control what they can and cannot do, based on risk, reversibility, and impact. Trust design involves defining boundaries, controls, and human oversight to ensure AI systems operate safely and within acceptable risk limits.

**Why it matters:**  
AI systems are probabilistic and can fail unpredictably. Guardrails prevent harmful outcomes in production environments.

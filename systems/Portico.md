Title: Portico — Fleet Design & Pre-Sales Optimization Tool

Idea: Portico is a cloud-based tool that helps Managed Print Service (MPS) providers design and optimize print fleets before a deal is closed. It connects to live pricing, quoting, and fleet operations systems to simulate cost efficiency, device utilization, and site coverage — giving sales teams a data-driven basis for scoping contracts.

Why it matters: Pre-sale fleet design tools reduce the risk of over- or under-scoping long-term service contracts. By grounding proposals in real device data and pricing, Portico enables accurate deal structuring and improves win rates by demonstrating measurable ROI to customers.

Connections:

- [[MPC]] — Portico feeds fleet design outputs into MPC for formal quoting and contract creation
- [[TMC]] — Portico reads deal details from TMC/CBA to validate transition project asset data
- [[Fleet Operations]] — Portico pulls live asset details from Fleet Operations for accurate design inputs
- [[Enablement Service]] — Portico queries BIRD (via fleet ops integration) for historical usage data
- [[Paladin]] — Portico uses Paladin for user authentication and role-based access
